Georgii Oblapenko's music

A fully documentation of all released music, organized by project.

Kunstmord

Song-based output falling under a very general umbrella of "post-rock", whatever that might mean.

Die Tür ins Nichts

A lot of instrumental parts recorded in the summer of '21, even before the release of the second record, as I knew I would not be able to bring a lot of musical equipment with me to Germany. Some pieces use samples dating back to 2013 or the like. Spent 2022-2023 trying to bring some structure to the whole project, recording bass parts, before giving up. In 2024, picked it up again, spent 2025 polishing and adding synth overdubs, writing lyrics, recording vocals and mixing.
Released on 28 February 2026.
Bandcamp

Le Noir Officiellement Pur

Started working on this almost immediately after releasing the first record. A lot of inspiration originally came from some of the dark ambient sounds from the soundtrack of "Twin Peaks: The Return". Most of the tracks written concurrently with the recording process, although "Filth" is based on some very old riffs I'd had since 2012 or so. Instrumental to "Jack" recorded fully in Austin TX on a cheap electric guitar I bought during COVID, played through a broken amp I picked up on the street. Vocals recorded in summer of '21.
Released on 10 September 2021.
Bandcamp

Coldmorgen Muzak

A collection of various songs I had recorded up to that point, the oldest ones ("Rainlake", "Savage") dating to 2012, and some other written and recorded in 2015/2016.

Collusionwave

A vaporwave record that originally started as an idea to combine 80s Soviet and 90s Russian pop-based vaporwave with noise rock, was subsequently abandoned, and then resurrected with additional tracks based on my experiences living in the US replacing the noise-rock parts (which were never recorded).
Released on 20 January 2025.
